Spanish Premier Division All-Time Table 1929-2025 (94 Leagues)

The historical classification of the First Division football in Spain is made by the Royal Spanish Football Federation and is based on points earned by each team in first division, whether single-season victory gave 2 or 3 points to the winner, or the number of points at stake.
S = seasons spent in Primera División 
P = points (In leagues witch two points and three points)
MP = match played 
MW = match win
MD = games drawn 
ML = games lost 
GF = goals scored
GA = goals conceded
